# POWERBI-GLOBAL-ELECTRONICS-RETAILER-ANALYSIS

# Description
This analysis explores month-wise and year-wise performance, product category contributions, profit margins, and purchasing patterns by customer demographics and AOV (Average Order Value) groups.

# Question: 

 Which AOV group contributes the most to profit and what does that imply?



# Key Findings: 

# Sales & Profit Insights

February reported the highest profit, followed by December and January.

Computers, Home Appliances, and Cameras & Camcorders led both in Net Sales and Profit.

Desktops, Televisions, and Projectors & Screens topped the charts for both Subcategory Sales and Profit.

Movie DVDs had the highest quantity sold at subcategory level, followed by Desktops and Bluetooth Headphones.


# Customer Insights

Elders are the most frequent purchasers, followed by Adults and Young Adults.

The online store had the highest customer count, followed by Northwest Territories and Kansas.

Orders peaked in 2019, followed by 2018 and 2020.


# AOV-Based Analysis


High Spenders contributed most to lifetime value, followed by Moderate and Low Spenders.

Interestingly, Low Spenders brought in the highest total profit, indicating a higher volume of smaller transactions.

  Categories like Audio and Music, Movies & Audio Books are mostly bought by low spenders, showing they have a small but loyal group of customers.  


# Findings:

 Computers are consistently the top-performing category across all key performance indicators.

  Low spenders bring in the most profit, so small purchases from many customers are important.  

  The average order value (AOV)has gone down each year since 2016, so it’s important to find ways to increase how much customers spend per order.  

  Music, Movies & Audio Books have high profits but low sales, so they are good choices for running special offers or promotions.  


# Recommendations

Offer bundles or discounts to low spenders to encourage them to buy more in one order.

Promote computers and desktops more, since they bring in the most sales and profit.

Create special offers for older customers, as they shop the most.


# Answer

Low Spenders contribute the most to profit, implying that bulk small purchases dominate the revenue stream.
